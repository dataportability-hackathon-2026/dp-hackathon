# Open Datasets

- [Open Humans](https://www.openhumans.org/) — consent-based personal data from real contributors (Fitbit, genomics, activity)
- [City of Austin Open Data Portal](https://data.austintexas.gov/) — civic and mobility data
- [HuggingFace Datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets) — search "persona" or "dialog" for additional synthetic data
